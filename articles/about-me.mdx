## Who am I?

I'm Matt. I build things for the web, mostly with React.

I didn't start out wanting to be a programmer. As a teenager, and then as a young man, the plan was a career in music. I
still play every day. But somewhere along the way I figured out that a career in music is about a lot more than playing
music, and I loved the music a lot more than the industry. Programming is where I ended up, and I feel lucky that I found
it.

## What I build

Here are some of the things I've built.

- <InternalLink href="/apps/elbowroom">Elbow Room</InternalLink> is a realtime chat room built with React and Firebase.
  For a long time a working chat app felt like the line between dabbling and being a real programmer, so I built one.
- <InternalLink href="/apps/lets-make-a-gif">Let's Make a Gif</InternalLink> is a photo and video editor with a Node.js
  backend and 19 ffmpeg filters. It's a nod to the stop motion videos my friends and I made as kids with my dad's video
  camera.
- <InternalLink href="/apps/password-generator">Password Generator</InternalLink> started out as a shell script I still
  use. It finally got a UI.
- <InternalLink href="/blog/color-tools">Color Tools</InternalLink> is a small npm package for converting colors and
  generating palettes.
- <InternalLink href="/misc">Tools and toys</InternalLink> has the rest, including the text generator I used for most of
  the headings on this site.

This site is built with Next.js, styled-components and Framer Motion. The cube on the homepage started out in
<InternalLink href="/misc/experiments-with-a-cube">Experiments with a Cube</InternalLink>.
